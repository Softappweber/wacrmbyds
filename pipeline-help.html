How to Use the Kanban Board?

A simple guide to managing leads visually

What is a Kanban Board?

A Kanban board is like a whiteboard with sticky notes. Each sticky note is a lead, and each column is a stage in your sales process.

You move sticky notes (leads) from one column to another as they progress.

NEW  →  CONTACTED  →  INTERESTED  →  CONVERTED
[Lead 1]   [Lead 3]    [Lead 5]     [Lead 7]
[Lead 2]   [Lead 4]    [Lead 6]     (Customer!)

What Do the 5 Columns Mean?

🔵 NEW: Leads you just added. You haven't contacted them yet.

Example: Someone filled your form 5 minutes ago.
🟠 CONTACTED: You've sent them a message or called them.

Example: You sent a WhatsApp message introducing your business.
🟣 INTERESTED: They replied and showed interest.

Example: They asked "How much does it cost?" or "Tell me more".
🟢 CONVERTED: They became a customer! You made a sale.

Example: They paid for your product or service.
🔴 LOST: They said no or stopped responding.

Example: "Not interested" or no reply after 3 messages.

How to Use the Board

1Look at the board

Open the Pipeline page. You'll see all your leads arranged in 5 columns.

2Find a lead

Each card shows the lead's name, phone number, and company.

3Drag the card

Click and hold a lead card. Drag it to another column. Drop it there.

4Status updates automatically

When you drop a card in "Contacted", the lead's status changes to "Contacted" automatically.

Real Life Example

Let's say you're a real estate agent:

1New Lead: Ramesh fills your form asking about apartments.

His card is in the NEW column.

2You Contact Him: You send a WhatsApp message with apartment details.

Drag Ramesh's card to CONTACTED.

3He Shows Interest: Ramesh replies "I want to see the 3BHK apartment."

Drag his card to INTERESTED.

4He Buys: Ramesh pays the booking amount. 🎉

Drag his card to CONVERTED.

Pro Tips

Tip 1: Check your board every morning. Focus on INTERESTED leads first — they're closest to buying.
Tip 2: If CONVERTED column is empty, you need to contact more NEW leads.
Tip 3: If LOST column is growing, your message or offer needs improvement.
Tip 4: Use the WhatsApp button on each card to quickly message a lead.

Quick Actions on Each Card

Every lead card has two buttons:

  • WhatsApp button (green): Opens WhatsApp with that lead's number
  • Edit button (blue): Opens the lead editor to change details

These buttons help you work faster without leaving the board.
