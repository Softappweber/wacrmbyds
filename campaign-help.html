How to Run a Campaign?

Step-by-step guide to successful WhatsApp marketing campaigns

What is a Campaign?

A campaign is a planned marketing effort to reach a group of people with a specific message and goal. Think of it like running a promotion in your shop — you decide what to offer, who to tell, and when to tell them.

Simple example: You own a clothing shop. You want to tell 50 customers about your "Buy 2 Get 1 Free" offer this weekend. That's a campaign!

Campaign Status Explained

  • Draft: You're still planning, not ready to send yet
  • Active: Campaign is running, messages are being sent
  • Paused: Temporarily stopped, can resume anytime
  • Completed: Campaign is finished, goal achieved or time ended
Example: You create a "Diwali Sale" campaign as Draft on Oct 1. On Oct 15 you make it Active. During the sale you Pause it for 2 days. After Diwali, you mark it Completed.

How to Create a Campaign (Step-by-Step)

1Click "Create Campaign"

On the Campaigns page, click the green "Create Campaign" button.

2Give Your Campaign a Name

Use a clear, memorable name.

Good names: "Diwali Sale 2025", "New Product Launch", "January Follow-up"

3Write a Description

Briefly explain what this campaign is about. This is for your reference only.

4Set a Goal

What do you want to achieve?

Examples: "Get 20 new orders", "50 responses", "100 leads converted"

5Choose Start and End Dates

When should this campaign run? Leave empty for no specific dates.

6Select Status

Start with "Draft" if planning, or "Active" if ready to go.

7Select a Template

Choose a message template you've created in the Templates page. This is the message that will be sent.

8Click "Create Campaign"

Your campaign is now created! Next step: assign leads.

How to Assign Leads to a Campaign

1Find Your Campaign

On the Campaigns page, locate your campaign card.

2Click the User-Plus Icon

This opens the "Assign Leads" window.

3Search and Select Leads

Use the search box to find specific leads, or click "Select All" to add everyone.

4Click "Assign"

Your selected leads are now part of this campaign.

Tip: You can assign the same lead to multiple campaigns. For example, a lead can be in both "Welcome Campaign" and "Diwali Sale".

How to Send Campaign Messages

1Click WhatsApp Icon on Campaign

Find your campaign card and click the green WhatsApp button.

2System Opens WhatsApp

WhatsApp Web will open for each lead one by one with your message pre-filled.

3Click Send in WhatsApp

You just click Send in each WhatsApp tab. The message is already typed for you.

Important: With wa.me (free method), you must click Send manually for each message. With Meta API (automated), messages go automatically without any clicking.

Campaign Examples (Real Life)

Example 1: Product Launch
Campaign: "New iPhone 16 Launch"
Goal: Get 100 pre-orders
Leads: All customers who bought iPhone 15
Message: "Hi {name}! iPhone 16 is here! Pre-order today and get 10% off. Reply YES for details."
Example 2: Festival Sale
Campaign: "Diwali Special"
Goal: 50 sales in 1 week
Leads: All leads in "Interested" status
Message: "Happy Diwali {name}! Our special Diwali sale starts today. Up to 50% off. Visit our store!"
Example 3: Follow-up Campaign
Campaign: "January Follow-up"
Goal: Convert old leads
Leads: Leads who haven't responded in 30 days
Message: "Hi {name}, we miss you! Here's a special welcome-back offer just for you."

Tracking Campaign Performance

On each campaign card, you can see:

  • Leads: How many leads are assigned
  • Messages: How many messages sent
  • Responses: How many leads replied
  • Conversions: How many became customers
Tip: A good campaign has high response rate. If responses are low, try changing your message or targeting different leads.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Mistake 1: Sending too many messages at once — this can get your number blocked.
Solution: Send in batches of 20-30 with gaps.
Mistake 2: Generic messages — people ignore "Dear Customer" messages.
Solution: Use {name} variable to personalize every message.
Mistake 3: No follow-up — most sales happen after 2-3 follow-ups.
Solution: Create a follow-up campaign after your main campaign.

Campaign Checklist

Before you launch, check:

  • ✅ Campaign name is clear
  • ✅ Message template is ready
  • ✅ Leads are assigned
  • ✅ Start date is set
  • ✅ Goal is defined
  • ✅ Message has {name} for personalization
  • ✅ You've tested with your own number first
