Automation Help Guide

Learn how to automate your WhatsApp marketing

What is Automation?

Automation means your CRM works for you automatically without you doing anything manually. Instead of typing and sending messages one by one, you create rules once, and the system follows them.

Example: When a new lead is added, an automated welcome message is sent to them instantly — without you clicking anything.

What are Auto-Responders?

Auto-responders are automatic replies sent when something specific happens.

  • When a new lead is added → auto-send a greeting
  • When lead status changes → auto-send a follow-up message
Real-life example: A customer fills a form on your website. They get added to your CRM. Instantly, they receive: "Hi {name}! Thanks for your interest. We'll contact you soon."

What are Scheduled Messages?

Scheduled messages are messages that go out at a specific date and time you choose.

  • Send a message tomorrow at 10 AM
  • Send a promotional message on a festival day
  • Send a reminder before an event
Real-life example: You schedule a message for Monday 9 AM: "Good morning! Our new offer starts today. Reply YES for details."

What are Drip Campaigns?

A drip campaign is a series of messages sent automatically one after another with time gaps.

  • Message 1: Sent immediately
  • Message 2: Sent after 24 hours
  • Message 3: Sent after 48 hours
Real-life example: Day 1: "Welcome to our service!"
Day 2: "Did you check our features?"
Day 3: "Here's a special offer for you."

What are Welcome Messages?

Welcome messages are the first message a new lead receives when they enter your CRM.

  • Make a good first impression
  • Introduce your business
  • Tell them what to expect
Real-life example: "Welcome to ABC Company! We're excited to have you. Our team will contact you within 24 hours."

What are Active Automation Rules?

Active Automation Rules are the rules you have created that are currently turned ON.

  • Active (Green): Rule is working and will trigger automatically
  • Inactive (Gray): Rule is saved but turned OFF, won't trigger

You can turn rules ON/OFF anytime using the toggle switch.

How to Create Automation Rules

1Click "Create Automation Rule"

Found at the bottom of the Automation page.

2Enter Rule Name

Give your rule a name you'll remember. Example: "Welcome New Customers"

3Choose Automation Type

Pick one: Auto-Responder, Scheduled Message, Drip Campaign, or Welcome Message.

4Choose Trigger

When should this rule run?

  • New lead added: When someone is added to your CRM
  • Status changes: When a lead's status changes
  • Specific time: At a date/time you choose
  • Manual: You trigger it yourself

5Select Template

Choose a message template you've already created in the Templates page.

6Set Delay

How many seconds after the trigger should the message send? (0 = instantly)

7Set Active to Yes

Make sure it's set to "Yes" so the rule starts working.

8Click "Save Rule"

Your automation rule is now active!

Quick Tips

Tip 1: Always test your automation with your own number first.
Tip 2: Use {name} in templates to personalize messages automatically.
Tip 3: Start with a Welcome Message rule — it's the easiest.
Tip 4: Don't create too many rules at once. Add one, test it, then add more.
